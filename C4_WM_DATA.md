# C4_WM_DATA — the C4 world-model training data (configs/c4_wm.toml)

Owner rule: **no designed controllers in the world-model training data.** The mix was
intended to be native driving plus RANDOM direct-output CAV actions only.

> **Correction (DATAHYG, 2026-10-14; see DATAHYG_NOTES.md).** The mix below does NOT satisfy
> the rule. (a) The switching pack's `direct-mix` arm is not random direct-output only: every
> CAV switches per its seeded schedule among {native, CIRCLES damping, direct}; damping is
> 19.4 % of direct-mix CAV time (52 CAV-h) and at least one CAV is damping somewhere in 99.7 %
> of the 120 s direct-mix TRAIN windows. (b) The dspeed trajectory arm contains the scripted
> segment types `merge_aimed` and `gap_opening` (designed behaviours) besides random target /
> slowdown / speedup ramps, and the loader labelled every CAV of a dspeed window `direct` with
> the realized acceleration as its "command" (only 57 of 1118 dspeed CAV-h are directed).
> `data.controller_hygiene = "strict"` (configs/c4_wm_v4_hygiene.toml) removes both; the
> configs c4_wm / v2 / v3 / v4 keep the original data (switch default off).

1. `data_cache/pack_v3` — TRAIN windows of the `native` / `native_base` arms. The
   lpwo_v3 noPI/noWall lists already exclude PI and wall arms; the C4 selection
   additionally drops the damping `base` arm of those lists. `switching*`, `fixed*`
   and `w-*` arms never enter the lists, and all CALIBRATION items are excluded.
2. `data_cache/pack_dspeed` — all four sampled target-speed trajectory arms
   (`arm0..arm3`), TRAIN split.
3. `data_cache/wmn1_switching_train` — the `direct-mix` arm only (per-CAV switching among
   native / CIRCLES damping / random direct-output; see the correction above); the
   `switching` arm (which adds PI segments) is excluded. The zero-shot held-family guard (family 3) is enforced with the usual
   `drop` policy; the 30 held-out worlds are refused.

Selection lists are generated by `tools/make_c4_lists.py` into
`runs/active/c4_wm_lists/` (registered as `c4_wm_lists`): 583 native TRAIN windows
(pack_v3 139 + pack_dspeed 444), 99 validation windows (23 + 76), and an explicit
TRAIN-scoped 10%/seed-0 world split of the 90 direct-mix windows (81 train / 9
monitoring).
