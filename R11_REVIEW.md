# R11_REVIEW - independent design review of round 11 (S-R11REV, standing in for astra), 2026-10-24 ~02:00
Read-only review of branch `c4/v10r11` at ef66de15. Read: packet, design, config, gate .sh and `wms/train/round11_gate.py`, the `model/follower_courtesy.py` v2 code, the `wms.py` and `cutin_reaction.py` diffs, `losses/follower_courtesy.py` (contrast), `docs/r11_offline_fit.json`, `docs/r11_weight_rule.json`, the lr-1.0 micro-smoke log, `tools/r11_checks/run_all.sh`, `tools/r10b_checks/analyze.py`, R10B_REVIEW and the R10B offline json.

**Verdict: GO-WITH-CHANGES.** Nothing in the code is unsafe or leaks. Launch with **R11_HEAD_INIT=zero** (C2) and add the head-saturation gate rule (C1, ~6 lines). The main remaining risk is the absolute over-prediction in the last 3 s (finding 4). It is structural, the head-zero ablation cannot repair it, and the only fallback is round 10b itself. So it needs a registered decision rule (C3) before exec.

## Findings
1. **Saturation.** At lr 1.0 one Adam step moved the head mean by -37.7 (the plant range is [-9, +3]), so the gradient was 0 at step 2. Adam's first steps scale about linearly with lr. Checkpoint mode (head lr 5e-3/3e-3) is about 200x below that one-step lr; zero mode (5e-2/3e-2) is about 20x below it. With zero init only the last layer moves at step 1. On the exact production schedule offline, zero x1000 gives p0.1 -2.39, p99.9 +1.96 and share < -3 = 0. Safe enough, but the margin is thin and nothing checks it: the verdict module has no head-range check, and the 5b production-lr micro-smoke still says `RESULT_5B` (its train_log is empty). Add the gate rule (C1).
2. **Init: zero.** On the packet's own data zero init is better on everything except the lr-rule loss, where it trails by 0.012 (0.578 vs 0.566), and that loss was measured on a stand-in head. Zero init is closer to recorded excess at every event time and on dA 6-15. It is also better in ABSOLUTE terms in the last 3 s: +0.98 vs +1.13 (recorded -0.02). The real 10b head is more extreme than the stand-in (telemetry -3.9..+4.7 vs p0.1/p99.9 -2.1/+3.6). The x100 scale was chosen on the stand-in, so with the real head the 90 steps may stop part-way through unlearning it, and the head would then be an uncontrolled mix of the old and new targets. Zero init gives clean one-change attribution: fix-B tensors from 10b, head trained only by the contrast. Caveat: the 9.067 weight was measured at a degenerate point (at a zero last layer the hidden-layer gradients are identically 0). The weight acts only through the global clip (grad_clip 1.0, active: smoke grad norm 6.4). Second-order; accept it.
3. **Headway vs gap matching: defensible only as a consistency choice, and not identified.** Changing the kernel coordinate changes the target 2x (-0.5 vs -1.2). The kernel estimator also does not match the registered cell evaluation. At convergence (long fit) the cell excess goes to about -0.06 vs recorded -0.32. So the near-recorded production numbers are a stop-point effect, and the converged contrast under-attributes. M1 is one-sided (it only rejects < -0.9), so it cannot see under-yielding. Matching on the evaluation's covariates also makes M1 partly circular. A further confound: the contrast controls are `lane1_rows & ~applies`, which still contains the fix-B post-insertion event-window rows. Their state (near the end, short headway) overlaps the partner rows in the last 2 s, and fix B itself excludes them (`~window`). Not blocking. Read it (C5).
4. **Absolute last-3 s bias: the real risk.** At convergence the partner-row prediction is `rec - r_hat`, so its absolute error equals the error on the matched controls. Offline (held-out, last 3 s): head0 +1.45, round-10b loss +0.33, round 11 +0.98 (zero) or +1.13 (from 10b). Round 11 is about 3x worse than 10b in absolute terms. Fix-B controls are dominated by rows far from the merge, so 90 steps are unlikely to remove the near-merge part. In rollouts, followers then accelerate into the gap: the frozen pivot sees smaller gaps, which means fewer merges and stuck ramp cars (R9_STUCK) or tighter insertions, harder braking after the cut-in and lower TTC. Zeroing the head makes this worse, not better. Also, the two offline tools disagree on the same nominal base: at -2 s the base is -0.01 in R10B (n 47) and +1.46 in R11 (n 217). Reconcile this before quoting "+1.2..1.6" anywhere.
5. **Causality: sound.** `a_ramp` = `kin[t,5]`, the post-step acceleration of the step t-1 -> t. `a_ramp_prev` = short-history slot -2; `initial_history` and `push_history` put t in slot -1, so this is t-1. The zone counter updates from the next state after each step, both in `rollout` (after `kin = state_kin(current)`) and in `one_step_replay`, and both initialise it from pre-t0 long history. Zone = road geometry [0, 75.44] m, consistent with physics. Targets enter only as labels. CAV ramp partners expose their applied acceleration (observable, as intended).
6. **Exact zero / CAV / one path: OK.** The `torch.where(has_partner & learned, raw, 0)` mask is unchanged. Partner rows and contrast controls are both HDV-only. `recorded_partnered` decides partners from s and lane alone, so it matches the head's pairing (tested). The check one-step path uses `one_step_replay` (carries the zone counter) and rollouts use `model.rollout`. Only propagators that call `step()` without the carry see t_zone = dt. No such caller exists today; it matters for C4 deployment later.
7. **Gate and reject criteria: mechanics sufficient, behaviour gaps.** The gate does not check the head range (C1). M8 is listed as a "read" but `tools/r10b_checks/analyze.py` (reused unchanged) computes only the C3-matched excess. Nothing computes the absolute last-3 s bias, the control bias or the contrast telemetry, and the main round-11 risk (finding 4) has no decision rule.

## Required changes
- **C1 (gate, tiny code, before launch).** In `round11_gate.check_log`, over every step that has courtesy rows, append an error (exit 3) if:
  - `follower_courtesy_head_min <= accel_min - accel_max` (-12), or
  - `follower_courtesy_head_max >= accel_max - accel_min` (+12), or
  - `follower_courtesy_head_mean` is outside (`accel_min`, `accel_max`) = (-9, +3).

  Any of these means the offset alone pushes rows past the plant clamp (dead head). Read the bounds from the config's physics. Add one verdict test using the lr-1.0 log (mean -37.6 must fail). This is a launch check on the model, not a clamp in it, so it is consistent with the owner rules. Apply the same rule to all 90 steps of the run log in M7: a violation means reject round 11.
- **C2 (launch choice).** `R11_HEAD_INIT=zero`, with R11_INIT left at its default (10b stage300, else best). The gate then passes weight 9.067 and lr x1000. Keep everything else as configured.
- **C3 (registration, before exec; implementation can follow the launch but must exist before the morning read).** Promote M8 to a rule and implement it (a small addition to analyze.py or a sidecar script):
  - Compute the absolute target-follower acceleration per event time over [-6, +2] s, model minus recorded, one-step and rollout, for 10b, round 11 and round-11 head-zero. Also compute the bias of the matched unpartnered controls near the merge.
  - **Keep round 10b (reject round 11)** if round 11's mean absolute bias over [-3, 0) exceeds 10b's by > 0.3 m/s^2 AND any of these is worse than in 10b: merge count, stuck ramp cars, TTC < 1.5 s count, minimum gap at insertion, post-insertion hard brakes.
  - M5 (collisions/TTC worse than 10b: reject the round) stays unchanged.
  - Add the under-yield read: flag if the M1 mean over [-3, 0) is > -0.1 (the yield effect has disappeared).
- **C4 (packet hygiene).** Fill or remove `RESULT_5B`. With C1 in place, the GPU gate smoke (2+2 production-lr steps from the real init) supersedes it.
- **C5 (morning reads, not gates).** (a) `r_hat` with vs without fix-B event-window rows among the contrast controls. (b) Whether the near-merge control bias falls over the 90 steps (telemetry: `follower_courtesy_contrast_control_resid_mean`). (c) The R10B vs R11 offline base discrepancy (finding 4).

Not changed: rollout_grad false, Huber 1.0, hidden 32, merge_pivot frozen, round-9 decisions, the fix-B weights, and no stochastic head (deferring it is reasonable).
