## Findings
1. **Driver params / prior.** The physical map `lo+(hi-lo)·sigmoid(centre+shrink·raw)` removes the 1.78/2.87 floors (tested). Zeroing head.2 rows 0-4 is exact: rows 0-8 are the means, 9-17 the uncertainties. The ranges are wide physical bounds, not tolerances. **The prior is not compatible with "learned only".** `driver_prior.py` names its targets "medians of the textbook population", and `tests/unit/test_infrav5.py` asserts they EQUAL `population.TEXTBOOK_POPULATION_MEDIANS`. That is generator truth by construction, whatever the literature provenance. It anchors exactly the quantity this round claims to learn (the population centre), and it makes BASE calibration against the same generator partly circular.
   - Cost of turning it off is small. Its production share at the encoder output is 0.003 (micro-smoke), and the rollout terms dominate.
   - Offline (one-step only, the weakest objective), no-prior does not collapse within the production schedule: a p10/p50 0.67/0.78, no rows at a bound. It is better on most classes, and free road +0.30 vs +0.47 is the difference between passing and failing BASE-2.
   - The collapse seen in S-BASEDIAG's longer, higher-lr one-step fit is a real identifiability risk. Guard it with an evaluation rule (C1), not with a truth anchor. If a collapses, that is a finding to report, not something to mask.
   - **Weak (0.01): not recommended.** It keeps the provenance problem and is nearly inert.
   - Per-driver variation survives but is narrow (a p10-p50 spread of about 0.1). Drivers without history sit at the centre, which lies above most observed drivers (1.27-1.44 vs median 0.78-1.06). Entrants in rollouts will therefore be the more eager drivers; read it in ENC. C2 (per-driver identification) is not demonstrated by this round.
2. **Trainable set / residual / temporal.** Freezing the gate follows R10_DIAG and is right. The residual re-init is exact (row 0 only, live gradient). At x0.5 (2.5e-5) the residual can rebuild only a modest output in 110 steps, which is acceptable because the core should carry the motion.
   - **The gap is temporal.** Packet reading 4 shows the round-9 temporal term attached to the calibrated core gives free road -0.93, braking +1.09 and all-row MAE 1.09. That is worse than round 9, and it would fail BASE-2 and BASE-3.
   - The lr rule fitted the centre WITH this frozen term, so the "core" columns are the target only if temporal unlearns about 0.5-0.9 m/s^2 gated.
   - At x5 (2.5e-4 / 1.5e-4) round 10 moved temporal by 1.7 % of its norm in 90 steps. No evidence shows it can move this far. Meanwhile the centre (x1000) is the fast learner and may absorb the stale offsets instead.
   - Zeroing temporal is not a free fix either: its anti-persistence (slope -0.66) damps long rollouts.
   - So: launch as configured and read early (C2). Clip 1.0 with every step clipped means Adam on a normalised gradient, so the per-parameter step is about lr. The position share of 0.80 is the intended objective for a base retrain.
3. **Courtesy v2 on the calibrated base.** "Contrast ≈ absolute" holds only if the matched controls are unbiased. 86-89 % of the controls sit in the zone/approach, where the calibrated core still shows +0.89 (approach/zone slow start).
   - At convergence the partner's absolute error equals the control error (R11_REVIEW finding 4). The head may therefore learn little of the hold, and BASE-4's "±0.3 with head" is optimistic.
   - Mitigation in the data: the offline +0.89 was measured with hidden junction leaders, which fix D removes from the contrast replay. The size of that effect is unmeasured.
   - The micro-smoke control residual (-0.58 / -0.63 at init) is the telemetry to watch (C3).
4. **Fix D: correct.** `replay_present` = filled presence ∧ lane, so the "new"/reset logic no longer re-initialises transit vehicles. Supervision still comes from `_recorded` (loss_valid); pairing and matching use the replay state; it is causal.
   - The caveat (s frozen about 2 steps for junction occupants) leaves a 3-8 m gap error instead of about 58 m hidden. That is a net gain, it touches only the courtesy contrast, and all check arms are replayed alike.
   - The offline expectations (hidden presence) are not directly comparable to the filled-presence checks. That is fine because the comparison is relative to round 9.
5. **Merge pivot / support.** The 24-window recount (0/24 differ) shows only that the geometry class is base-independent, which holds by construction. The motion class (15/14,676) is unmeasurable at that size. This is sufficient pre-launch, given that the pivot is frozen and the exclusion is recomputed per batch. The registered 192-window post-run recount plus the merges/stuck CIs (in both directions: slower discharge can also overshoot) cover the rest.
6. **Schedule.** The packet uses round 9's 190 s/step, which had no courtesy replay. Round 11, which has the replay, measured median 228 s / mean 200 s at stage 120 (R11_LAUNCH). With 300 s steps slower still, the projection is about 7-7.5 h of training, plus about 1 h final validation and about 1 h smoke: **about 9 h**, not 7.8. `max_hours` is not enforced in run mode, so there is no truncation risk, only wall time (C4).
7. **Gate / checks: mechanics complete.** Init report, strict reloads, bitwise frozen set, centre moved, cut-in absent, prior mode.
   - BASE-1/2/3, M5/M8, seed set 2, per-region offsets and round 9 re-scored are all registered.
   - Gaps: no mid-run read (C2); the M5 wording "AND/OR" is ambiguous (C5).
   - Lane path: the packet's claim "every lane-side input stays round 9" is inaccurate. Driver params feed MOBIL `lane_base`, and lane rows 5-8 drift through the shared head.0 at x10 with no loss on them (lane share 0). Under `lane_learnable=true`, feasibility is geometric and the v5 head overrides learned rows, so the impact should be about zero. Read it (C5).
8. **TTC / near-miss.** Lower a and b change IDM in two ways:
   - Closing-speed braking `(v·dv)²/(4 b s²)` gets stronger as b falls, which is good for TTC.
   - Zero-dv cut-in braking `a(s*/s)²` halves with a. Softer post-insertion braking is closer to SUMO (R10_DIAG -2.3 vs -0.6), but insertions may end tighter.
   - Slower queue starts open lane-1 gaps that the frozen pivot will license, so expect more merges at smaller insertion gaps.
   - M5 (collisions, TTC<1.5 s) and M8 (min insertion gap p5, post-insertion hard brakes) cover this. Keep M5 as the hard rule.
