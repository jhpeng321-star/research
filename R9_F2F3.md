## F3 root causes and repairs

1. **Scene membership versus supervision.** Previously, the recorded replay received
   only the t0-cohort alive mask, ownership and t0 lengths. Neighbours arriving later,
   including CAVs and reused slots, disappeared or inherited zero/stale length. In
   first-passage mode, C4 and original-pack loaders now provide `merge_pivot_scene`
   with current full-scene presence, ownership, continuity, lengths and current actions.
   The supervision cohort remains a separate mask. Replay resets longitudinal history,
   recurrent state and encoder/controller identity at continuity breaks. A focused test
   demonstrates a later CAV follower being restored with its current 7 m length.

2. **Future-dependent preprocessing.** `junction_fix=ffill` interpolates positions using
   a later on-lane row. First-passage loaders now bypass that interpolation and its t0
   drop policy. `causal_scene` carries only a previously observed same-identity lane;
   it retains the recorder's observed route coordinate verbatim. It never interpolates
   or invents junction progress. Junction-born identities without a prior lane remain
   unknown observations and retain the strict scene veto. Window and prefix anchors
   use the same adapter. Tests alter future positions and verify unchanged current inputs.

3. **Different native CAV plants.** A timeline previously made recorded replay use
   default physics for native CAVs, while runtime could use encoded/frozen forward
   acceleration. First-passage callback state now supplies the actual native forward
   acceleration, including identical stop-time integration. Available current timelines
   select the same controller path in training/runtime even with legacy `cav_drive=teacher`;
   future teacher motion is excluded. Direct current commands and immutable CAV insertions
   retain their existing strict semantics. Other decision modes retain their old path.

4. **Wrong comparison geometry.** Rebuilding on-policy geometry from target lengths and
   only recorded K slots loses actual generated-entrant lengths/neighbours. Runtime now
   emits its pre-commit full-scene neighbour geometry. Comparison consumes it directly;
   synthetic legacy fixtures retain their reconstruction fallback. Tests forbid target-
   metadata reconstruction when captured runtime geometry is present.

5. **Bumpers and all-neighbour veto.** Verified front positions occupy `[s-L,s]`;
   follower clearance subtracts ego length, leader clearance subtracts leader length.
   These conventions were already correct and were not shortened or offset by 2 m.
   Every target vehicle remains in the exact strict-intersection check, not just nearest
   neighbours. Touching remains the zero-intersection boundary. Geometry now exposes
   blocker/neighbour indices, fixed-insertion veto and validity for audit explanations.

### Remaining vetoes: evidence, not a permissive patch

All **17** unsupported local HDV events have exact projected body intersections;
**3** also involve fixed-CAV target occupancy. There are zero reservation failures at events.
Diagnostic successor observations show:

- **16/17** also intersect in the recorded successor's *projected* body intervals.
- **7** blockers' successor rows are junction rows, whose recorded s is frozen.
- **1** blocker leaves the target lane during the transition.
- **1/17** intersects only under the model-predicted motion projection.

These counts overlap; they do not establish 16 real SUMO collisions. The frozen model
frame collapses junction length and carries the last on-lane coordinate: see
`traffic_shepherd/c4/geometry.py` and `traffic_shepherd/c4/state.py`. Thus even recorded
scalar positions do not always determine physical occupied intervals across a junction.

Concrete retained counterexamples:

- Seed 963004, vehicle 132, authorizing t=348.5 s: ego front **75.440002 m**;
  predicted target-CAV front **76.912956 m**, length **5 m**, leader clearance
  **−3.527046 m**. The CAV's successor is a junction row with frozen recorded front
  **74.333519 m**. Replacing the model motion by that row would not supply valid physical
  support; a lossless junction coordinate is missing.
- Seed 963007, vehicle 213, t=367.5 s: predicted ego front **0.899769 m** and
  target vehicle 157 front **5.329621 m**, length **5 m**: clearance **−0.570148 m**.
  The blocker moves to lane 0 in the recorded output. Exempting it using that output
  lane would leak future membership. The current scheduler uses pre-change occupancy;
  changing to an outgoing-moves-first scheduler requires causal ordering evidence and
  a shared runtime/replay implementation, not a recorded-lane exception.

No padded clearance, shortened nominal body, future-position substitution, relaxed veto,
mass floor, timing threshold, event deletion or F1 noise term was introduced.

## F2 posterior contract

Raw full-scene prefixes stop at t0; decisions replay only intervals ending at or before
t0, and the t0 anchor supplies continuity only. Current-command coverage and observed
pre-risk entry certify an identity. Prior absorbing exits, slot reuse, missing commands,
unknown observations, or a junction-born/truncated entry cannot invent certification.

The exact sufficient statistic is the eligible pre-window running maximum `C_entry`.
Window event score uses `log(F_event-F_previous) - log(S_entry)` over the sequence;
wait/censor scores telescope similarly. Frozen prefix geometry is detached but its
boundary maximum and survival denominator are recomputed with current parameters at
each recorded TBPTT chunk, preserving all seven analytic gradients. Exact infinities
remain exact. See `F2_PRE_WINDOW.md` and the focused tests for API/lifecycle details.
