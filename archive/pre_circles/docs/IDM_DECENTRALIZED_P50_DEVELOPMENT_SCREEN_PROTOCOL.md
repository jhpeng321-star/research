# Proposed IDM Decentralized p50 Development Screen

Status: approved, implemented and executed on development-only seeds. The
first-onset integrity screen passed, but the pooled comparative retention gate
failed. P50 is therefore not retained for broader development, policy
training, or predictor work. Results and the stop decision are recorded in
`IDM_DECENTRALIZED_P50_DEVELOPMENT_SCREEN_RESULTS.md`.

## Scientific question

The fixed p30 terminal-speed ceiling has physical authority and produces both
helpful and harmful outcomes, but its fresh harmful tail is too large and its
benefit is not observable from the current actor state. The p50 hypothesis asks
whether a less severe terminal speed differential preserves useful wave
damping while reducing delayed safety and service harm. It changes terminal
target only. Braking-rate authority, safety projection, option duration,
release, candidate placement and control remain unchanged.

The 20-second predicted-dose admission is not part of this screen. Existing
outcomes show that it discards 27.8% of lower/transition opportunities while
only modestly changing the qualified harmful fraction, with inconsistent
demand-specific behavior. The frozen admission remains 16 seconds so that the
screen isolates the p50 target.

## Matched treatment definition

- Development-only seeds: 4121--4132, already inspected and permanently
  excluded from independent claims.
- Initial cell: 2000 veh/h mainline with the frozen 400 veh/h ramp.
- Initial landmark: first persistent `risk_onset` only.
- Candidate: the same closest admissible runway-ranked CAV used by the p30 arm.
- Control: native IDM abstention.
- Replay: a second exact native-IDM abstention branch.
- Comparator: existing fixed p30 terminal-speed ceiling.
- Candidate treatment: lane-disturbance p50 terminal-speed ceiling.
- Requested duration: 20 one-second controller steps.
- Adequate dose: at least 16 command steps.
- Outcome horizon: 60 one-second steps.
- CAV penetration: 10% scattered CAVs.
- Hard comfort, jerk, TTC, secure-gap, SUMO safe-speed, option latching and
  smooth-release behavior: unchanged.
- No configured-demand input, lane locking, stronger braking, handoff, wall or
  future-outcome feature is introduced.

The p50 arm must be implemented as a new named action set. It must not overwrite
or silently reinterpret any p30 artifact or checkpoint. Discovery and branch
fingerprints must record the percentile action set.

A diagnostic-only full-option shadow has already established mechanism
distinctness on the initial 12 decisions: all 221 p30 command states were
covered, 33.0% of projected commands differed from p30 by at least 0.05 m/s2,
and six cases exceeded a 30% distinct-step fraction. This satisfies the reason
to run a real p50 arm, but not any treatment-dose, safety or efficacy criterion,
because the shadow states were generated by p30.

## Frozen implementation slice

The implementation is limited to:

1. a p50 action-set constant and single active mode in
   `idm/decentralized_damping.py`;
2. p50 reference and target-specific rear-gap admission in
   `env/idm_residual.py`;
3. explicit p50 CLI admission in
   `tools/run_idm_decentralized_authority_verification.py`;
4. unit tests for decoding, target percentile, safety invariance, action masks
   and unchanged p30 behavior;
5. a matched p30/p50 outcome analyzer that reports common-case contrasts.

No observation, reward or policy-training change is included in this slice.

The implementation also freezes matched-treatment replay: later P50
recurrences consume the already recorded P30 decision state and exact P30
candidate identifier. This prevents a gentler P50 rear-admission rule from
moving the decision time or substituting a different candidate. Missing P30
recurrences remain missing; they are not replaced by a different event.

## Initial smoke pass/fail

The first-risk-onset, 12-seed screen passes integrity only if:

- discovery, branch and abstain-replay identity are exact;
- every branch completes its horizon;
- controls issue zero damping commands;
- treatment commands target only the declared CAV;
- zero collisions and teleports are added;
- at least 80% of p50 treatments deliver at least 16 of 20 steps;
- at least 90% pass safety and service constraints excluding dose;
- p50 command traces differ from p30 beyond replay noise in at least 30% of
  adequate-dose cases.

Failure of any integrity, dose or safety item stops expansion and triggers a
mechanism diagnosis. Passing these items establishes only a valid treatment,
not efficacy.

## Development efficacy decision

If the initial smoke has valid treatment dose, extend p50 only to the already
opened second and third 2000-veh/h risk onsets. On common qualified cases, p50
is retained for broader lower-loading development only if:

- its qualified harmful fraction is lower than p30;
- its qualified tenth-percentile effect is higher than p30;
- its mean qualified effect is no more than the frozen 0.02 deadband below
  p30;
- helpful and nonbeneficial outcomes each occur in at least three seeds; and
- at least 30% of qualified effects exceed replay noise.

These are comparative development criteria, not independent validation. If p50
passes, repeat paired development analysis at 1900 and 2100 on already opened
seeds, then reevaluate observation sufficiency and reward alignment. If p50
fails, do not search percentiles on fresh seeds; the honest next pivot is a
conservative abstention/operating-domain controller or a redesigned low-
authority treatment.

The executed pooled screen failed because P50 tied P30 at four harmful cases
out of 24 common qualified cases instead of producing a strictly lower harmful
fraction. Per the frozen rule, the 1900/2100 expansion, percentile search,
reward changes, policy training and predictor work were not run.

## High-loading interpretation

The 2200 and existing 2700 cells are not pooled into the p50 selection screen.
Their negative p30 mean/tail behavior and poor dose-threshold response are
recorded as residual high-loading insufficiency. After a damping checkpoint is
independently validated, these states become candidates for counterfactual
escalation to damping-preserving moving-wall control.
