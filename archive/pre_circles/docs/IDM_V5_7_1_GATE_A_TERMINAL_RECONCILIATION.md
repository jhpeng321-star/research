# IDM v5.7.1 Gate A terminal reconciliation

Status: **completed artifact-only reconciliation; no SUMO replay was run.**

This document corrects the formal horizon interpretation in the frozen v5.7.1
Gate A without changing its corridor, seeds, controller, decision time, or
outcome definitions. The original `complete_horizon` field treated any
termination before t=1058 as incomplete. That is too broad: a simulation can
end normally after all demand has cleared without losing a scientific outcome.

## Strict terminal rule

A branch is a complete terminal observation if it either reaches the requested
common horizon, or ends by verified natural clearance. Natural clearance
requires all of the following recorded evidence:

- SUMO reports `terminated`, not gridlock or an error;
- final live-vehicle count is zero;
- terminal expected/pending vehicle count is zero;
- no collision or teleport is recorded;
- the final branch summary contains discharge and delay/service accounting.

For a paired damping replay, the two branches must additionally have exact
replay identity and the same terminal classification, reason, and final SUMO
time. An arbitrary early terminal event remains incomplete.

## Result

All four damping pairs have exact replay identity. Seed 4402 reaches t=1058.
Seeds 4401, 4404, and 4405 terminate naturally at t=1036, t=1037, and t=1051,
respectively; both members of each pair end identically with zero remaining
and expected vehicles. Therefore **Gate A passes under the verified
natural-clearance terminal rule**.

| Seed | Terminal classification | General congestion headroom | Sustained-collapse headroom |
| --- | --- | ---: | ---: |
| 4401 | natural clearance at t=1036 | 36 s | 0 s |
| 4402 | requested horizon at t=1058 | 91 s | 91 s |
| 4404 | natural clearance at t=1037 | 21 s | 0 s |
| 4405 | natural clearance at t=1051 | 100 s | 100 s |

General congestion headroom means a nonzero frozen congestion-predicate
duration. Sustained-collapse headroom means at least 60 consecutive seconds.
They are distinct: 4401 and 4404 are valid general-congestion cases, but they
do not support a collapse-prevention claim.

## Consequence

The correction authorizes only the separately versioned v5.7.2
upstream-convergent feasibility gate. It does not reinterpret v5.7.1’s
all-seed formation veto as a treatment success, and it does not authorize a
predictor, fresh seeds, or a wall outcome branch until the v5.7.2 feasibility
gate passes.
