Status: **frozen before any v5.4 treatment outcome is opened.**

Date: 2026-08-31

The original v5.4 fixed-p25 availability stage failed on seeds 4333--4352 and
stopped before a wall branch was opened. A prior independent treatment-blind
v5.3 scan had shown that 30% penetration materially increases full-lane compact
placement support (40 windows across 18 seeds versus 24/16 at p25 in that
cohort). Therefore this amendment predeclares one higher operating point, p30,
without changing the v5.4 treatment, safety envelope or outcome gates.

The same seeds 4333--4352 and decision steps 620/660/680/700 are scanned at p30.
Their p25 placement records are allowed provenance; no v5.4 outcomes exist.
The p30 scan must independently satisfy at least 20 admitted windows across 14
seeds and two decision times. If it fails, wall availability is a no-go for the
present 10--30% domain. If it passes, the exact p30 scan freezes the admitted
starts and authorizes only the bounded paired v5.4 support pilot.

Every integrity, dose and independent-seed outcome-diversity gate remains
identical to `IDM_WALL_V5_4_EMERGENCY_HANDOFF_SUPPORT_PROTOCOL.md`. Before any
p30 treatment outcome was opened, the Gate-B label was also made explicitly
safety-first: a meaningful benefit is a frozen hard-brake-onset,
hard-brake-exposure, low-TTC, or merge-discharge improvement; delay is only a
service-noninferiority guardrail and queue area is diagnostic. The versioned
contract is `idm_escalation_gate_b_safety_service/1`. This is the only
operating-point/label amendment permitted before a treatment outcome is opened.
