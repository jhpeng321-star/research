## Autopsy

The stronger tax **overshot abstention**: instead of converting structural-harmed seeds into
ties, it suppressed intervention on seeds the policy was previously winning — the count fell
from 33 back to exactly the v9 baseline of 27, with 6 net seeds lost and structural harm
unchanged-to-slightly-worse (10 vs 9 harmed).

Interpretation: the easy-intervention tax does not distinguish "interventions that hurt"
from "interventions that win"; at 0.25 it penalizes both, and the policy chooses coast in
situations where acting was actually optimal. The tax lever is at or past its optimum at
0.15. Direction of the dose-response is now measured: 0.15 → 33/50, 0.25 → 27/50. No
reason to explore 0.30+; a 0.18–0.20 midpoint is possible but low-expected-value given
the shape (the win comes from G-axis behavior, not more abstention).
