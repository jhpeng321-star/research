## 0. What the project is (one paragraph)

SUMO highway-merge CAV research. The headline is **WM-N1 "Idea A"**, a controller-in-the-loop, strategy-agnostic neural world model:
- CAVs are moved by the real controller code;
- HDVs are predicted as an IDM/MOBIL baseline plus a neural residual over neighbour kinematics, with no strategy labels and no CAV flag;
- it predicts delay / TTC<3 / hard-brake over 300 s for ANY CAV plan.

It's used to (1) predict unseen controllers (J4 → J4B, wall law v2.1 → v2.2), (2) predict unseen incidents and pick the best response (INC1), and (3) drive a planner (PL-WM1).

## 6. Known pitfalls

- Windows files have CRLF: always LF-normalise before hashing. `sha256sum -c` with CRLF lists fails, so `tr -d '\r'` first.
- The AutoDL boxes have no `python3`/`zstd` on PATH: use the venv python and Python `zstandard` for decompression.
- Laptop → box upload runs at ~5–8 MB/s, laptop → Google at ~0.7 MB/s; box ↔ laptop over SSH is fine.
- Gates and preflights have repeatedly passed on synthetic data while the real path was broken. **Always verify on the real pack with per-step loss logs before GPU time** (memory: gates-must-use-real-data-path).
