## Claim and horizon boundaries
- **C1-FACTOR SUPPORTED architecturally:** executable CAV control can be separated from HDV dynamics. Historical teacher forcing does not demonstrate autonomous feedback, and historical residual-mode settings were inert in physics; do not attribute its win to learned-settings identification.
- **C1-PI SUPPORTED only historically; current extension NEEDS A RUN:** old teacher-forced PI 60 s summary 16.9/23.8/28.2 m is not a full-history current-model comparison. Preserve the adverse refresh, rather than silently promoting PI to a successful 13E/WM2 closed-loop result.
- **Historical 120 s WALL error:** `wall_same` medians 99.84/152.46/245.44 m; WM−BB-traj **−49.21 [−110.05,−7.31] m**, 66 starts/29 worlds, supports relative endpoint advantage despite high absolute error. `wall_pilot` **−38.92 [−82.12,+0.60]**, inconclusive. No historical wall 180–300 s score exists in this receipt.
- **WM2's 120–300 s merge/queue failure does NOT invalidate C1 as stated at historical 60 s:** it changes checkpoint, population and feedback/queue protocol. It DOES block a claim of generally accurate current closed-loop long-horizon controller evaluation. A model can beat BB error while miscalibrating absolute levels and selection.
- `E2_direction_horizon.md` supports its registered sign test at 10/30/60/90 s, fails at measured 120/180 s; it does not bind a checkpoint alias or prove a 300 s verdict. r13e/r14b/r16 remain NOT-QUALIFIED overall. Do not transfer a “90 s trusted horizon” automatically to WM2 or read it as a full-model safety/magnitude certificate.
- Existing evidence therefore supports a **60 s C1 headline**, historical teacher-forced endpoint comparison through 120 s where explicitly significant, and separately labeled exploratory 13E traffic/selection diagnostics through 300 s. No matched WM2-versus-BB 120–300 s output was established here.
