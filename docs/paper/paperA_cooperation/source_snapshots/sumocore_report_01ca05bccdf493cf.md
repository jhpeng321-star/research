# S-SUMOCORE — observable-start SUMO is better at traffic fields, not yet an action oracle

**Answer:** the motion-inferred variant is closer on speed fields and absolute-delay scores, but neither variant reproduces the full traffic distribution well enough to choose actions. Defaults give better sample-mean controller-effect signs. There is no overall qualified winner.

## What ran
- SUMO 1.27.1, initialized at300s from observable IDs/positions/speeds/lanes, geometry-derived routes and observed entrance counters; no original saved state, driver truth or assignment files.
- Eight geometry-supported WMDATA states:969421/422/425/426/427/428/432/435; native/MAPPO169/PI × default/inferred settings ×3 paired futures, scored at120/300/600s. Six development and two reserved worlds remain separate in the receipts; this is a small diagnostic, not an untouched qualification set.
- Inference uses the frozen existing60s physical encoder SHA5b256941: v0/31.29→speedFactor, headway→tau, gap→minGap, acceleration/braking directly to IDM. LC2013 stays at population medians. Future arrivals, CAV fraction and inferred entrant settings are estimated/sampled from observables, not taken from the original future.
- SUMO reseeding alone is deterministic for this configuration (S-ENSPROBE); our spread comes from explicitly sampled future demand/entrants. It is not a measured same-state real-world randomness distribution.

## Distribution and action results
- Lower CRPS means a better distribution forecast. Across all three arms, default delay CRPS at120/300/600s is **1,087/5,452/19,343 veh-s**; inferred is **647/4,635/16,728**; demand×penetration×arm climatology is **1,433/4,375/9,131**. Inference helps strongly at120s but loses to the conditioned baseline at300/600s.
- On the exact16 MAPPO/PI world-arm cells with saved WM2 forecasts, WM2 CRPS is **865/4,749/20,908**, default **1,112/5,637/20,125**, inferred **663/4,732/17,523**. These compare forecast systems with different encoder/sampling contracts, not a same-encoder ablation; WM2 has K8, SUMOCORE K3. Fair-K scores are also saved.
- Upstream speed-field CRPS at120/300/600s: default **1.27/1.60/3.03 m/s**, inferred **0.47/0.80/1.34**, conditioned climatology **1.49/1.59/2.07**. Inference gives substantially more realistic speed evolution; all10s fields/support are retained, including congestion clearing.
- Over supported bins,600s merge/ramp speed CRPS improve more modestly: default **7.27/3.77 m/s**, inferred **6.80/3.74**. Region-specific missing support is retained in the score files rather than filled with artificial speeds.
- At300s, pooled native/MAPPO/PI upstream detected-breakdown rates are default **88.9%**, inferred **84.7%**, real **62.5%**. At600s they are **98.6%/87.5%/87.5%**. Matching the last pooled rate does not fix timing: both-event onset MAE at600s is **134s default versus204s inferred**; onset/censoring/pre-existing mass is scored separately.
- Historical WM2 **96.2% versus77.8%** native300s breakdown is a different36-world panel, not a matched rate comparison here. The same registered BIC detector is used; its dominant downward step is a proxy, not proof of a physical phase transition.
- Both variants cover the real600s absolute delay in only **20.8%** of K3 ensemble ranges (exchangeable reference50%). Default pooled600s bias is only−49veh-s, yet its CRPS is poor: opposite errors cancel. Inferred bias is+6,575veh-s. A good mean or one matched breakdown rate is insufficient.
- At600s, real sample-mean MAPPO/PI delay shifts versus native are **−2,064/−3,858 veh-s**. Default predicts **−2,325/−5,024**; inferred predicts **+3,450/+825**, getting both point-mean signs wrong. Inferred event-probability shifts are0/0 versus real+12.5/−12.5percentage points. Eight-world uncertainty is broad; these are not resolved population-effect claims.
- At600s, development-only delay CRPS default/inferred/climatology is **21,437/19,225/10,447**; reserved-two is **13,061/9,238/5,180**. Neither split provides long-horizon distributional qualification.

## Validity, accounting and implication
- **Physical-validity FAIL:**18/144 futures, all from969425 across both variants/all arms, report a collision during the first movement at300.00s. Every initial ID/lane/position/speed audit passed; the shared exporter independently agrees on649 initial vehicles/routes/5m lengths. Initialization/internal-state loss remains unresolved, so this is not a clean information ceiling.
- All18 flagged futures stay in the scores. Six full trajectories were recovered after post-run assertion failures, without re-simulation and with explicit float32 rounding bounds; independent trajectory reintegration matches published road clocks within6.31e−6veh-s. Four zero-step failures and two capacity-limited partial runs are preserved; no finished rollout was repeated or tuned away.
- **144/144 complete;87,517.5 actual sim-s**, including1,117.5 partial-run seconds, below100,000. Conservative precharge90,150. Representation capacity rose192→512 only after exact frozen-MAPPO padding parity on all8states; no driving parameter changed. All own supervisors exited; no training, GPU use, push or peer-process signals.
- Verification:4contract+3score tests pass; independent full144-run trajectory/source/pairing/accounting audit PASS; shared targets match54 factual clock values exactly. Statistical bands resample10,000 whole worlds within development/reserved strata; tiny cells and omitted finite-K Monte Carlo bands prohibit a powered-gate PASS.
- **WM rebuild implication:** physics plus motion inference is a promising short-horizon traffic prior, but accurate lanes/IDM alone are not an established600s action selector. Repair observable initialization and driver/entrant inference, then judge timing, coverage and expected controller shifts jointly. This experiment neither proves microscopic prediction impossible nor establishes that replacing the learned merge stack alone fixes the WM.
