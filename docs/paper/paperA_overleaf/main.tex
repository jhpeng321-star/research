\begin{abstract}
MAPPO-style multi-agent learning can teach connected and automated vehicles
(CAVs) competent individual driving while missing rare cooperative moves.
Walls, gap creation and metering may pay off minutes later: random exploration
rarely finds the coordinated moves, while noisy team returns obscure learning.
We propose a physics-informed world model (WM) as an exploration guide.
From observable state, it compares joint moves with policy/baseline actions
under the same sampled hidden futures and proposes likely delay-saving moves.
The model needs useful action rankings rather than exact future reconstruction.
All learning reward and advantage come from real SUMO execution.
Existing exact-model planning establishes
headroom: it saves
% source: E:/agent-work/c4-coopplan/docs/paper_results/COOPPLAN.md:22--27.
$648.407$ $[209.699,1173.616]$ and $1583.821$ $[588.246,2765.414]$
veh-s/world at the J600 and J900 clocks on $36$ development worlds.
Starting from MAPPO, use importance-corrected PPO and trust-weighted imitation;
proposal trust decays adaptively as SUMO evidence favors the policy's own
actions. Compare with frozen MAPPO and same-budget continued training, and
test whether cooperation persists after trust reaches zero.
\pending{learned gains, proposal advantage/trust and exact deployed synergy /
S-COOPMWM; insert outcome sentence only after complete evidence.}
\end{abstract}

\section{Introduction}
% Editorial allocation: I 0.60 page; front matter 0.35 page.
\para{Cooperation pays late.}
A CAV that slows down may initially worsen its own progress while creating a
gap, metering an arrival stream or helping stabilize a cross-lane wall.
The group benefit can emerge minutes later. A shared traffic reward must
assign that delayed benefit amid changes caused by hidden human behaviour,
new arrivals and other agents. Competent individual driving therefore need
not discover rare coordinated interventions through random exploration.
This is a task-specific exploration hypothesis, not an impossibility theorem.
In an existing, deliberately oversampled TRAIN corpus, helpful options occur in
% source: E:/agent-work/c4-coopbandit/docs/paper_results/COOPBANDIT.md:7--18.
$45/300$ states: $15\%$, with a $95\%$ world-bootstrap interval of
$[10,20.33]\%$. Only $81$ states have an eligible group.
This is corpus prevalence, not an on-road event rate.

\para{Guide exploration; grade it in SUMO.}
A physics-informed WM compares interventions under shared uncertain futures,
without reproducing the one future that happened. Common random numbers (CRN)
can cancel background variation in predicted savings. Promising proposals
guide early exploration; real SUMO supplies all reward and advantage.
Adaptive trust withdraws guidance as the policy catches up. Exact joint,
member-alone and baseline twins separately assess excess-over-solos synergy.
\pending{guided discovery, measured noise cancellation and autonomous
cooperation after trust withdrawal / S-COOPMWM.}

\begin{itemize}

\section{Related Work}
% Editorial allocation: II 0.45 page. All keys have inherited verification receipts.
\para{Counterfactual credit.}
Difference-reward lineage~\cite{wolpert_tumer2002}, COMA~\cite{foerster_coma2018}
and difference-reward policy gradients~\cite{castellini_drreinforce}
establish credit by counterfactual subtraction. Our group excess-over-solos
quantity differs from an agent's marginal-in-team contribution and from a
Shapley allocation~\cite{han_shapley2022}. Contextual counterfactual planning
also measures cooperation~\cite{barnett_counterfactual2026}. The target
contribution is long-horizon guided exploration, SUMO grading and trust
withdrawal. Here synergy is an evaluation estimand, not a new reward.

\para{World-model imagination.}
Dreamer-style imagined learning is relevant
\citeneeded{Dreamer primary paper; no verified inherited entry}.
MACD, the Multi-Agent Counterfactual Dreamer, is direct prior art for
model-based counterfactual credit~\cite{chai_macd2024}.
Physics-informed traffic MARL is likewise established~\cite{liu_physics_wm2026}.
Here the WM proposes actions, while transitions, reward and advantage come
from real SUMO, avoiding direct optimization of imagined reward.
Action-response validity still needs testing~\cite{oefinger_validate2026};
biased proposals can waste real budget, and ranking alone cannot establish
synergy or live improvement.

\para{Mixed-autonomy cooperation.}
MAPPO~\cite{yu_mappo2022}, on-ramp MARL~\cite{chen_merge2021} and
Flow~\cite{wu_flow2022} provide the learning and mixed-traffic lineage.
CIRCLES~\cite{lee_circles2024} demonstrates open-road wave-control context;
AV coalition rewards~\cite{han_shapley2022} provide cooperation incentives.
Platoon and merge coordination are related mechanisms
\citeneeded{dedicated platoon reference if a specific prior claim is added}.
Coordinated appearance and aggregate return do not by themselves establish
excess-over-solos benefit.

\section{Problem Formulation}
% Editorial allocation: III 0.65 page.
\subsection{Partially Observed Multi-CAV Control}
Define a variable-agent Dec-POMDP with latent traffic state $x_t$, transition
law $P$, active CAV set $\mathcal A_t$, causal local histories $h_{i,t}$,
RSU observations $z_t$, and shared traffic reward. A decentralized actor uses
local history and an observable RSU broadcast; a centralized critic is used
only during training. Hidden driver behaviour and future arrivals are
uncertainty, not actor inputs. The WM samples a fitted/prior-conditioned
future $\xi\sim q(\xi\mid h,z)$ rather than the realized hidden future.
Residual speed and time-gap setpoints are anchored to MAPPO commands; lane
behaviour and the safety executor retain the inherited plant contract.

\subsection{Source-Inclusive Team Delay}
For a baseline/intervention $S$, use a speed-deficit delay integral
\begin{equation}
J_H(S)=\int_t^{t+H}\!\left[\sum_{j\in\mathcal V(\tau)}
\left(1-\frac{v_j(\tau)}{v^{\rm free}_j}\right)_+
+Q_{\rm src}(\tau)\right]\,d\tau .
\label{eq:cost}
\end{equation}
Here $\mathcal V$ contains all on-road vehicles and $Q_{\rm src}$ counts
requested vehicles waiting to enter. Future entrants and source waiting
remain in the team objective, in veh-s; lower is better.
Scoring references and source requests are not additional actor observations.
\pending{bind exact evaluator free-speed and source-wait convention /
S-COOPMWM.}
Distinguish intervention duration $W$, replay horizon $H$, and episode score
clocks. The existing planner's
% source: E:/agent-work/c4-coopplan/docs/paper_results/COOPPLAN.md:14--15,22--30,73--84.
J600/J900 clocks start after a $300$ s warmup; event credit uses H120 or,
in separately reported panels, H300.

\subsection{Exact Cooperation Estimand}
Freeze group $G$, background $B$, joint proposed targets, nonmember actions,
intervention window and continuation. In branch $S\subseteq G$, only members
of $S$ issue their joint-proposed targets; other group members follow $B$.
Solo branches retain joint targets and context rather than replanning.
\begin{align}
D_H(S)&=J_H(B)-J_H(S),\\
\cg&=D_H(G)-\sum_{i\in G}D_H(\{i\})\label{eq:synergy}\\
&=\sum_{i\in G}J_H(\{i\})-J_H(G)-(|G|-1)J_H(B).\nonumber
\end{align}
Beneficial cooperation requires $D_H(G)>0$ and $\cg>0$. Positive synergy
alone can coexist with a harmful group. For larger groups, $C_G$ aggregates
beyond-singleton interactions; it is neither a Shapley allocation nor intent.
Member-removal marginals $J(G\setminus\{i\})-J(G)$ are not solo effects.
% source: E:/agent-work/c4-coopnative/docs/paper_results/COOPMWM_DESIGN.md:21--28;
% E:/agent-work/c4-papera/docs/paper/paperA_cooperation/CLAIMS.md M-DEF.
The earlier member-revert reward-credit arm is superseded.
\pending{bind coalition-alone exact diagnostic/discovery exports and their
declared baseline to the redesigned policy; no WM reward is used.}

RSU-observable situations while including ordinary ones. The fixed model is

\subsection{Replay Fidelity and Noise Diagnostics}
For branch costs $X,Y$,
\begin{equation}
\operatorname{Var}(X-Y)=\operatorname{Var}(X)+\operatorname{Var}(Y)
-2\operatorname{Cov}(X,Y).
\end{equation}
For the synergy contrast with coefficient vector $a$, variance is
$a^\top\Sigma a$. CRN can reduce variance when paired branches share
positively correlated variation. It does not remove model bias or guarantee
that most noise cancels. Compare CRN replay, independently sampled replay
and real team-return advantage at matched starts/groups/horizons, reporting
raw variance, standardized signal and world-level uncertainty. Zero or biased
contrast is not useful merely because its variance is small. Separate
conditional WM-estimated noise from actual PPO-advantage dispersion across
real windows: their sampling distributions differ.
% source: E:/agent-work/_sol/coopmwm_prompt.md:26--28,49; COOPMWM_DESIGN.md:55--58.
Exact playback checks action order, $D$ and coalition-alone $C_G$ at
$60/120/300/600$ s, with signs, scale, ties and world uncertainty.
The earlier fidelity-gated multi-horizon reward and credit ramp are superseded;
diagnostics no longer gate B training. Any multi-horizon proposal weighting
requires a registered ablation.
\pending{proposal fidelity and variance reduction preserving exact signal.}

\subsection{SUMO-Only PPO under a Proposal Mixture}
At sampled eligible windows, execute a WM proposal with probability $\beta$;
otherwise use the policy. Elsewhere use policy exploration. For joint behaviour
law $\mu_\beta$ and proposal law $q_{\rm WM}$, write
\begin{align}
\mu_\beta(a\mid h)&=(1-\beta)\pi_{\rm old}(a\mid h)+\beta q_{\rm WM}(a\mid h),\\
 r_\theta(a,h)&=\pi_\theta(a\mid h)/\mu_\beta(a\mid h).
\end{align}
PPO uses clipped importance ratios against the actual behaviour mixture.
All reward and advantage come from SUMO's source-inclusive team delay;
neither WM delay nor $\widehat C_G$ enters reward. Add imitation toward
executed WM proposals with weight proportional to $\beta$.
\pending{common reference measure and proposal likelihood, including
deterministic atoms; joint-density correction under masks/execution mapping;
loss normalization and imitation coefficient.}
A Gaussian density at a deterministic proposal is not its mixture mass.

Full-prefix exact SUMO reruns check no-op/trajectory parity, controller memory,
shared exogenous coupling, joint/solo identity and source bookkeeping.
Exact twins supply diagnostic labels, not actor features or WM-based reward.
Arm A matches policy capacity, causal inputs, base exploration and real-SUMO
budget; proposal guidance and its imitation are the treatment.

\section{Discussion and Limitations}
% Editorial allocation: VII 0.25 page.
Ranking is sufficient only for the decision/contrast being used. Delay order
does not guarantee synergy subtraction, calibrated probabilities or unbiased
contrasts. CRN cancels shared variation only to the extent branches covary;

Positive synergy with harmful group delay is not beneficial cooperation.
