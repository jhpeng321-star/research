# S-WMBREAKDOWN — judge traffic distributions, choose actions by expectation
**Answer:** yes: a useful WM should reproduce conditional traffic/breakdown distributions and choose actions by expected outcomes, not reproduce every realized breakdown. Current WM2 fails distributionally too; irreducible uncertainty does not explain away its bias.

- Baselines never use evaluation labels: development leaves the entire target world out; evaluation uses only the 32 development worlds. All 40 WMDATA worlds were previously used by WMCALIB: this is not a historically untouched qualification panel.
- Reused the exact registered LRDIAG BIC downward-step+trend detector (SHA `6de4df03…`): full-traffic 10 s vehicle-time mean speed, 60 s prehistory, upstream/merge/ramp. Observable raw/converted means agree within 3.6e-15 m/s; 4,500 decisions independently rechecked.
- No behavioral speed threshold. A detected dominant speed-loss step is **not proof of a metastable phase transition**. Pre-existing steps are separate; final 30 s cannot supply new onsets. Re-selecting the dominant step at each horizon means these are not nested breakdown events or estimates of a memoryless per-step hazard.

Saved cumulative/30 s delay components and action summaries contain **no speed/position trajectories**: imagined BIC breakdown rates/timing/controller shifts and speed-field distributions are unavailable. ## Proposed preregisterable distributional qualification gate
1. **Freeze before any fix evaluation:** model/rules, action interface and entry/driver sampling, identity-bound worlds and fresh evaluation split, observable state groups, native/CTRL0/MAPPO/PI/CIRCLES, 12 demand×penetration cells, horizons 120/300/600, the unchanged BIC detector, all metrics/baselines below. Use world-cluster uncertainty; arm/future rows are not independent worlds. Exact-world MAE is secondary.
2. **Rate calibration:** for every primary upstream cell×arm×horizon, ensemble-mean event rate must lie inside the simultaneous 95% real Wilson intervals, with interval half-width ≤10 pp; include WM Monte Carlo uncertainty in paired WM−real CIs and require compatibility with zero. Report pre-existing mass separately. Bonferroni α=.05 over 12×5×3=180 comparisons; failure/missing precision in any primary group prevents qualification. Merge/ramp are a separately corrected diagnostic family.
3. **Timing:** at each fixed horizon compare the discrete detected-onset CDF on 10 s bins, including a no-detected-onset censored category and a separate pre-existing category. Require WM CDF within simultaneous real world-bootstrap bands and upper simultaneous 95% CI of the paired integrated-Brier-score difference versus cell×arm climatology <0; never assign an artificial exact onset to a censored run. Use 10,000 whole-world bootstrap resamples, seed 20261031, with max-stat correction over each frozen metric family.
4. **Traffic distributions:** CRPS for team delay and each registered region's 10 s mean-speed field must beat both pooled and cell×arm/time-bin empirical climatology: upper simultaneous 95% world-bootstrap CI(WM−baseline)<0 at every primary horizon. Include source/road delay components, speed support/missingness and joint speed-field energy score diagnostically; no pooling that hides a failed demand cell. Check randomized-rank PIT and ensemble-range coverage against simultaneous exchangeability bands, not an arbitrary spread target.
5. **Action usefulness:** estimate controller−native and controller−CTRL0 shifts in expected delay and event probability within each frozen cell/state group. Predicted shifts must lie within simultaneous real paired CIs; every real sign resolved away from zero must agree, and lower 95% world-bootstrap within-group arm-order concordance must exceed 0.5 for both quantities. Unresolved signs stay unresolved; too few informative comparisons means insufficient evidence, not PASS. Do not require individual-world effect signs.
6. **Data/power:** one factual outcome per world suffices for group calibration, not same-state irreducibility. Under a calibrated shared-state Bernoulli planning model, K=32 and n≥341 independent worlds/cell give approximately ±10 pp simultaneous rate precision; **n≈517 (round 520) worlds/cell × 32 futures/arm** gives 80% power for any specified 10 pp bias after the 180-comparison correction. These are normal planning approximations, not guaranteed power under clustering/model heterogeneity: freeze final n after exact/world-bootstrap pilot power checks, with no optional stopping on evaluation.
7. **Expectation/conditional power:** for paired mean effects, n≥ceil[(z(1−α/(2M))+z(.8))² σ²/δ²], using frozen pilot paired-world SD σ and the smallest relevant effect δ; marginal α=.05, δ=.2σ needs about 197 worlds/group. Increase K until within-future Monte Carlo variance/K ≤10% of between-world paired variance. Same-state probability ±10 pp needs about 100 independent compatible real continuations/state and about 100 WM futures at worst-case p=.5; K8 alone has per-state binomial SE up to 17.7 pp.
8. **Current WM2 score:** NOT QUALIFIED. New-panel rate/timing/speed-field/native-shift gates are **unscorable** without trajectories/native predictions; historical global rate diagnostic fails. Delay coverage and evaluation cell-conditioned delay CRPS fail; effect CRPS fails in point estimates at all horizons and evaluation group expectations mis-sign MAPPO/PI at 600 s. Sparse cells cannot certify the proposed simultaneous rate/shift gates. This is a retrospective diagnostic, not a newly preregistered acceptance result.

## Mechanism and cheapest discriminating next check
- **Demonstrated: (a)-type distributional model error. Possible: (b) conditional unpredictability; hence (c) is plausible, but its shares are unidentified.** There is one real continuation per observation here. Hidden heterogeneity, future arrivals and chaotic amplification can limit exact outcomes; this dataset cannot prove outcomes are intrinsically random or that merge/lane-change physics is the sole cause.
- “Follow the physics” should target observable gap acceptance/lane-change/merge braking, discharge and entrant/controller continuity with physically faithful rules and compatible latent variability; test their induced event rates, onset distributions, speed fields and **expected action shifts**. Matching one pooled breakdown rate, adding noise or fitting a scalar delay offset can all pass a superficial check while choosing the wrong action.
- Cheap sequence: use these saved-score failures to reject current long-horizon qualification now; for a future authorized candidate, save small 10 s observable region-speed bins and delay components for a fixed pilot of worlds×futures, screen gross bias before a powered gate, and expand K only for Monte Carlo precision. The already registered S-ENSPROBE can inform same-state variability; do not duplicate its dispatch. No such simulation or inference was performed here.
