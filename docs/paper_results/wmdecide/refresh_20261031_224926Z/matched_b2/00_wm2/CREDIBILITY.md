Uncertainty: pointwise 95% percentile intervals from 10,000 stratified whole-world resamples (seed 20261031); arms and pairs are never independent replicates. These CIs condition on the saved ensemble and do not include finite-K resampling or shared model error. No simultaneous gate is implied.

Bootstrap composition is fixed within split/demand/penetration strata of the scored worlds. Singleton strata supply no resampling variation; a panel of distinct singleton cells cannot support these CIs even if its point scores differ.

Central 50/80/90% empirical-quantile coverage is descriptive at K8. Inclusive min–max coverage has continuous exchangeability reference 7/9; ties may raise it. Wide coverage alone does not establish useful predictions.

Picker rule remains P(candidate beats incumbent) >= 0.8 and lower expected full-team delay. That registered confidence rule is not proof of calibrated probabilities; use the existing S-RB-EVAL qualification gate for adoption.

Selected-switch diagnostics evaluate only the action actually selected by that rule for each hypothetical incumbent. They use the selected direction's strict paired probability, including reverse directions and ties, rather than an arbitrary unordered-pair orientation. No-switch cases remain in the picker switch-rate/saving summaries but are not recoded as wins or calibration cases. All five hypothetical incumbents remain clustered within each world.

Recorded paired-arm outcomes permit a retrospective original-state check; these picker savings are not closed-loop switching-policy performance or same-state probability identification. Real labels never enter selection. Probability/coverage/switch-rate values are fractions; delay scores and savings are veh-s. Positive saving helps the selected action; negative score differences favour the WM over climatology.
