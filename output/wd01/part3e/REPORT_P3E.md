## Diagnosis: the validation gate cannot be met

The p3c validation data (unchanged): at H=60 s, B2 (IDM normal) speed
RMSE 4.97 m/s with 0 collisions; B3 (oracle vType) 4.58 m/s with 0
collisions; B1 persistence 9.52 m/s.  The gate requires the model to
beat B2 (<= 4.97 m/s) with <= 1 collision/1000 vehicle-steps.

Any SUMO-emulating IDM-based model — deterministic or posterior-sampled
— cannot systematically beat a well-tuned IDM on data generated by that
same IDM unless it captures effects the IDM misses (lane-change
dynamics, controller actions, dispatch).  On this NATIVE data the p3c
baselines already include the full IDM physics, so the residual
learnable signal is only the lane-change and controller noise — which
the LC hazard and residual models cannot capture accurately enough to
improve on B2.

## Dawdle hypothesis: CONFIRMED

Per (vType, regime) one-step residual fit on 1.7M+ train pairs
(results/dawdle_fit.json):

| vType | regime | A_theory=sigma*accel*dt | A_fit | mean theory | mean observed |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| aggressive | following | 0.90 | 0.86 | -0.45 | -0.426 |
| aggressive | free | 0.90 | 1.60 | -0.45 | -0.784 |
| normal | following | 0.52 | 0.47 | -0.26 | -0.230 |
| normal | free | 0.52 | 0.74 | -0.26 | -0.366 |
| cautious | following | 0.20 | 0.27 | -0.10 | -0.132 |
| cautious | free | 0.20 | 0.40 | -0.10 | -0.199 |

The SUMO dawdle form is CONFIRMED in the following regime: the mean
residual matches -sigma*accel*dt/2 within 0.02-0.04 m/s for all three
vTypes, and A_fit ~ A_theory.  In free driving there is a consistent
excess (A_fit ~ 1.8x A_theory), suggesting an additional desired-speed
damping or a SUMO internal near-v0 effect not captured by the basic
IDM.  The adopted emulator uses A_fit (following) and A_fit (free) as
separate per-(vType, regime) uniform dawdle widths.

## Posterior: exact likelihood fails, prior is near-optimal

The exact generative likelihood over ~60 consecutive steps fails
badly: model log loss 10.58 (train) / 11.32 (val) vs prior 0.79-0.80,
accuracy 0.55 vs prior 0.70.  Root cause: ~1% of steps have residuals
outside the uniform support (lane changes, junction hops, braking
events), and their -inf likelihood dominates the product, making the
posterior degenerate.  The discriminative p3d classifier (accuracy
0.695) and the prior (0.703) are equally uninformative.  vType is
essentially unidentifiable from <=10 s of (s, v, a) history in this
data.

Consequence: ensemble members should sample vTypes from the PRIOR
(0.1/0.7/0.2), not from a posterior.
