## 1. Variant specs and provenance

All four are implemented in `partA_v2g/engine.py` behind the injected `lc_variants` section with the default OFF; unit tests in `partA_v2g/tests/test_lc2_variants.py` (13 tests, green): V-a fires only after 4 consecutive qualifying steps (and resets on a broken chain); V-b blocks discretionary changes for exactly 4.0 s after any change while mandatory merges stay ungated; V-c applies zero threshold + 1.0 m/s^2 bias from the zone start with the safety test unchanged; the flags-off engine path is identical to the nominal parameters.

- **V-a persistence:** A discretionary change fires only if the MOBIL criterion (incentive + safety) has held continuously for T_persist = 2.0 s (4 steps at dt 0.5). Implementation: a per-(vehicle, target-lane) consecutive-qualifying-step counter in the engine's discretionary loop; any failing step resets it; firing resets the vehicle's counters. Anti-flicker / decision-persistence device; value = 4 integration steps, chosen a priori, NOT fitted.
- **V-b manoeuvre time:** After ANY lane change the same vehicle may not start another DISCRETIONARY change for T_lc = 4.0 s (the change itself stays instantaneous; mandatory merges are NOT gated). Implementation: the discretionary candidacy gate uses max(nominal cooldown, T_lc) on the last-change time; merge candidacy keeps the nominal cooldown only. Provenance: empirical lane-change durations are reported at around 4-5 s (Toledo & Zohar 2007 - exact citation TO VERIFY per the registration's flag); value chosen a priori, NOT fitted.
- **V-c strategic early merge:** An accel-lane vehicle applies the mandatory-merge rule from the START of the merge zone (candidacy window already spans [zone_s0_m, zone_s1_m] = [378.66, 454.10], unchanged) with ZERO incentive threshold, plus a merge bias b_merge = 1.0 m/s^2 added to its MOBIL gain toward main_right. The safe-gap tests (lead headway, follower s0, b_safe(u) both sides) are UNCHANGED (test-pinned). Provenance: on-ramp bias convention of Treiber/Kesting-style MOBIL implementations; value chosen a priori, NOT fitted.
- **V-abc:** all three combined.

## 4. Pre-registered decision rule, applied verbatim

Registered rule (section 4 of the registration, quoted): a variant is a **BIG IMPROVEMENT** iff benefit AND no-harm hold. Benefit: (a) s_RMSE at H=30 AND H=60 each >= 20 % lower than nominal with the world-bootstrap 90 % CI of the relative change excluding 0, OR (b) disc-LC ratio at H30 in [0.67, 1.5] AND merge-LC ratio at H30 in [0.67, 1.5]. No harm: H10 and H30 s_RMSE not worse than nominal by more than 5 %; H30 delay MAE not worse by more than 10 %; collisions <= nominal + 0.1 per 1000 veh-steps. Otherwise MODEST if benefit is only partly met and no-harm holds, NO IMPROVEMENT otherwise.

Operationalization (documented, fixed before running): 'relative change' = (variant - nominal)/nominal of the window-mean s_RMSE (the summary.json convention); 'CI excluding 0' counted only when the point estimate is an improvement; 'partly met' = at least one RMSE sub-condition (one horizon at >= 20 % lower with its CI excluding 0, or both horizons >= 20 % lower without BOTH CIs excluding 0) or exactly one of the two H30 ratios inside its band; no-harm point estimates as registered (the rule attaches no CI to them), with the collision clause evaluated at H30 (and reported at every H in the table above, all within the nominal + 0.1 bound).

Reading: no variant reaches even one full benefit component. The best RMSE movement (va -8.8 % and vabc -8.4 % at H30, CI excluding 0) is far from the required -20 %, and both leave the H30 LC ratios essentially unmoved (disc ~4.4x vs required [0.67, 1.5]; merge 0.35-0.43x vs required [0.67, 1.5]). V-b slightly WORSENS s_RMSE. V-c moves merging in the registered direction (H30 merge ratio 0.37x -> 0.46x, early merges and H10 merge counts up, dwell down at H30/H120) at a small collision cost (0.029 -> 0.035 per 1000 at H30, still <= nominal + 0.1) but nowhere near the ratio band and with no RMSE benefit. V-b's manoeuvre-time block mostly reorders which vehicle fires (the block suppresses the second change of a pair within 4 s) and does not reduce the production rate.

## 5. Recommendation

**Keep textbook (owner option 1).** No variant is a BIG IMPROVEMENT, so per the registered recommendation rule option 2 is NOT worth registering on this evidence, and no smallest BIG variant exists. The residual disc over-production and short-H merge lag remain characterized model error of textbook MOBIL/merge at dt = 0.5 s (DIAGNOSIS_LC1.md section 6, option 1).

Caveat (registered up front): TRAIN-only evidence with a priori values. A variant adopted later needs fresh evaluation worlds, because C4L1 consumes the chunk-4 CAL for the nominal model. Nothing was adopted or selected here; params_nominal.json is unchanged.
